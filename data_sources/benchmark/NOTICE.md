# Benchmark construction inputs and attribution

These compressed files are source inputs to question construction. They contain no model predictions or experiment summaries. Their uncompressed hashes and expected paths are recorded in `manifest.json`. They are not covered by a blanket relicensing of upstream content.

- **CIViC**: evidence items and assertions fetched on 2026-06-09; CC0 1.0. Source: https://civicdb.org/ and https://civicdb.org/releases/licensing. Cite the CIViC project and retain source evidence identifiers.
- **BioASQ**: the four BioASQ 12B golden test batches, `12B1_golden.json` through `12B4_golden.json`, from the 2024 release. BioASQ distributes its data under CC BY 2.5: https://participants-area.bioasq.org/datasets/. Cite Krithara et al., *BioASQ-QA: A manually curated corpus for Biomedical Question Answering*, Scientific Data 10 (2023), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02068-4, and the BioASQ challenge. The benchmark filters these batches to 19 questions and normalizes their fields and answer contracts. Snippets and cited articles retain their source provenance.
- **SIGNOR**: the archived `signor_latest.tsv` used for construction, identified by checksum rather than a fabricated release tag. SIGNOR data is CC BY 4.0: https://signor.uniroma2.it/about/. Cite the SIGNOR database and preserve relation identifiers and PMIDs. The builder filters supported protein interactions and normalizes effect/mechanism labels.
- **Open Targets**: Platform 25.03, released 19 March 2025. The cached disease/target/direct-association slices originate from https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/opentargets/platform/25.03/output/. Platform data is marked CC0 1.0; its upstream-source licensing information remains relevant: https://platform-docs.opentargets.org/licence. Cite Open Targets.
- **IntOGen**: `IntOGen-Drivers-20200201.zip` and `IntOGen-Cohorts-20200201.zip`. IntOGen data is CC0, separate from its pipeline code license: https://www.intogen.org/faq. Cite the IntOGen compendium and preserve cohort identifiers.
- **ClinVar**: the archived, cancer-filtered somatic submission subset used by the constructor, obtained on 2026-06-15 from `submission_summary.txt.gz`. No named monthly source release was recorded, so the exact subset is pinned by checksum. ClinVar permits copying and asks for attribution: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/docs/maintenance_use/. Cite ClinVar and preserve variation identifiers.
- **Pathway Commons**: v14 HGNC GMT is obtained from its fixed official endpoint and verified by SHA-256. It is not bundled because Pathway Commons distributes data under its source-database terms: https://www.pathwaycommons.org/. The cancer-filtered construction uses INOH and NetPath pathway memberships; preserve source labels. The full aggregate download is not assigned the project code license.

The released 571-record benchmark contains derived questions, normalized answer contracts, reference annotations and excerpts from the sources above. See `docs/data.md` for the source/field mapping. BioASQ attribution and other applicable upstream notices remain part of the data release. The project code license does not replace those obligations.
